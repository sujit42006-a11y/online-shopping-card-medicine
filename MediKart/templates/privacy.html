Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how information is handled in the MediKart college project prototype.

The application may store account details, delivery addresses, cart items, order information, and prescription workflow data in its MySQL database.

2. How Information Is Used

The stored information supports application features such as account login, medicine shopping, checkout, address management, order history, and prescription upload demonstrations.

3. Prescription and Medical Information

MediKart is a college project prototype. Do not upload real prescriptions or sensitive medical documents to an unsecured classroom or demonstration deployment.

4. Data Security

Appropriate security measures are important for protecting personal information. This prototype should not be assumed to provide production-level security.

5. Data Sharing

Information handling depends on how the application is configured and deployed. Avoid entering real personal or medical information into a public demo.

For questions about this college project, contact the project team or your project supervisor.
