ABOUT MEDIKART

Your Health, Our Priority

MediKart is a college project prototype designed to demonstrate a convenient and user-friendly online medicine shopping experience.

Developed using Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the platform brings medicine browsing, shopping cart management, delivery addresses, orders, and prescription uploads together in one application.

🎯 Our Objective

To develop a simple web-based system that demonstrates the essential workflow of online medicine shopping, from browsing products to placing and viewing orders.

💻 Our Technology

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: Python and Flask
Database: MySQL

🛒 What We Provide

Medicine browsing, user accounts, cart management, address selection, checkout, order history, prescription upload, and administrative features.

Our Vision

To create a well-organized digital platform that demonstrates how web technologies can support medicine shopping workflows.

Note: MediKart is a college-project prototype and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or prescription verification by a qualified professional.
